# WeChat Official Account analysis guide

Analyze the article as a relationship and navigation asset, not only a long piece of copy.

Return these optional `platformAnalysis` fields:

```json
{
  "titlePromise": [],
  "openingScene": [],
  "argumentChain": [],
  "evidencePlacement": [],
  "emotionalCurve": [],
  "shareReason": [],
  "followReason": [],
  "relationshipHandoff": [],
  "accountInfrastructureLinks": []
}
```

Distinguish subscription, search, recommendation, social sharing, homepage/menu navigation, and private-domain handoff. Do not attribute performance to a single entry without evidence.

Common candidate mechanisms include pain-point solution, scene dilemma Q&A, contrast/cognitive viewpoint, personal growth story, hotspot deep analysis, checklist/tutorial, and identity spokesperson. Do not treat these as guaranteed formulas.

Separate traffic content accounts, personal/IP accounts, enterprise operating/lead-generation accounts, and institution/service accounts. Their trust sources, share motives, conversion paths, and success criteria differ.

Do not hardcode article length, title score, keyword density, posting cadence, or opening-rate thresholds from anecdotal cases.
